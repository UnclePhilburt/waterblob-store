Safety & Guidelines

Read this manual before assembling and using your Water Blob®. These guidelines are required for every operator and every user.

This product is NOT towable.

⚠Safety Warnings

To reduce the risk of serious injury or death, follow every rule below without exception.

  • This is NOT a life-saving device — always use a USCG-approved life vest.
  • Never leave children unattended.
  • Use only under competent adult supervision.
  • Only 2 persons at a time — exceeding this limit increases the risk of injury.
  • Not for children under 6 years old.
  • Do not use if damaged or leaking air.
  • Do not use under the influence of drugs or alcohol.
  • Do not use if underinflated — this can cause entrapment, leading to serious injury or death.
  • Use only in water with a minimum depth of 8 feet, away from personal watercraft, docks, bridges, or hazardous objects.
  • This product is NOT towable. Never tow or pull the Water Blob® with any watercraft, vehicle, or person on board.
  • Do not drag across land or rough surfaces.
  • Must be properly anchored.
  • No aerial tricks or gymnastics. No flips or somersaults — landing on your head or neck can cause serious injury, paralysis, or death.
  • Jumping height increases risk. Use caution and common sense at all times.
  • Do not drag by D-Rings.

Use at your own risk.

⚙Assembly & Inflation

How to inflate

  1. Lay the Water Blob® flat on a smooth surface free of debris.
  2. Inflate using a leaf blower or a shop vac with a two-way switch. A 6.5 horsepower shop vac is recommended — that’s what we use.
  3. Takes about 10–15 minutes to inflate.
  4. Inflate to ½ to ¾ full of air.
  5. Slowly guide into the water while ensuring no sharp objects are underneath.
  6. Run anchor straps in a criss‑cross pattern underneath the blob — this keeps it steady and prevents it from shifting or rolling during use.
  7. Anchor using shock cord and one 50‑pound concrete bucket per anchor point. Crisscross the anchor points.

Recommended air pumps

  • Leaf blower
  • Shop vac with a two-way switch (6.5 horsepower recommended)

▶Product Use Instructions

  • Use only during daylight hours in a well-lit area.
  • Maintain at least 12 feet of open water space on all sides and 8 feet of clearance above jumpers.
  • Do not use under bridges, trees, docks, or any overhead obstructions.
  • Only competent adults should supervise use.
  • Only 2 people at a time — exceeding this limit may cause serious injury.
  • Ensure the blob is anchored properly before each use.
  • Check for leaks, damage, or improper inflation before each use.
  • Never swim under the Water Blob®.
  • Take breaks and avoid use when tired.

✓How to Use the Water Blob® Safely

  • The Water Blob® area must be supervised by an authorized staff member.
  • Life vests are mandatory and must be securely fastened.
  • Only two users at a time: one "bouncer" on the end and one "jumper".
  • Weight difference between users must be no more than 30–50 lbs.
  • A staff member must be stationed on the platform to supervise all jumps.
  • Jumpers must land bottom-first onto the blob.
  • When being launched, the rider must lace their fingers together and place their hands behind their neck to protect the head and spine on landing.
  • New jumpers must wait until previous users have exited the water.
Warning:
  • Reckless use can result in injury.
  • No flips, twists, or aerial acrobatics.
  • No alcohol or drug use while on the Water Blob®.
  • Check local laws for water sports regulations.
  • Have emergency contacts and first aid available.

✨Care & Maintenance

Do

  • Monitor air pressure — heat can cause expansion, possibly damaging seams.
  • If stored in a cool area, add air before use.
  • Inflate on dry land only — never inflate in water (risk of electric shock).
  • Check inflation levels before each use to ensure safety and extend product life.
  • Clean with mild soap and water or a gentle detergent.
  • Store in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight.
  • Use plastic trash cans or wooden crates to prevent insects or rodents from damaging it.
  • If punctured, contact us for repair advice.

Do not

  • Drag it on the ground.
  • Overinflate in hot weather.
  • Pull it out of the water using a vehicle.
  • Pull by D-Rings.
